Crispy Chicken Burger

A crispy, juicy chicken burger with a crunchy coating, fresh vegetables, cheese, and a creamy sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken breast
  • 1 burger bun
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 lettuce leaf
  • 2 tomato slices
  • 2 cucumber/pickel slices
  • 1 slice of cheese
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon ketchup
  • Cooking oil

Instructions

  1. Flatten the chicken breast to an even thickness.
  2. Season the chicken with salt, black pepper, and paprika.
  3. Set up a breading station with flour, beaten egg, and breadcrumbs.
  4. Coat the chicken in flour, dip in egg, and then coat with breadcrumbs.
  5. Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium heat and fry the chicken until golden brown and cooked through.
  6. Toast the burger bun lightly.
  7. Assemble the burger by spreading mayonnaise and ketchup on the bun, adding lettuce, tomato slices, cucumber/pickel slices, cheese, and the crispy chicken patty.
  8. Serve hot and enjoy your crispy chicken burger!
